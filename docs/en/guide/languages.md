# Languages and dark mode

## Arabic and English

- Turn on English in Salla's **Settings → Languages**. The language picker shows in the announcement bar.
- Arabic pages read right to left and English pages left to right, sliders and arrows included.
- Default texts in blocks and settings read in English on English pages. When you write your own text, fill both
  languages from the editor's language field.
- Product and category names come from Salla: add their translations in the dashboard.
- **Thawbi fonts for English pages** gives English a Latin font that suits the design.

## Dark mode

- **Default mode**: light, dark, or the visitor's device setting.
- The header's **mode switch** keeps the visitor's choice in their browser.
- Thawbi's blocks and store pages follow dark mode. Some ready-made Salla windows (reviews, size guide) keep their
  own colours.
